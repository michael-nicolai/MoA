Representative light micrographs (left and right panels) and averaged Raman spectra (centre) of feathers from extinct bird taxa, highlighting the putative melanin spectral region (900–1900 cm⁻¹). Spectra represent the mean of multiple Raman measurements collected from each feather. Columba livia feathers containing known eumelanin (black feather) and pheomelanin (brown feather) are included as reference standards for comparison with the remaining specimens.

Species are shown in the following order: (A) Rock pigeon (Columba livia), black feather; (B) Rock pigeon (Columba livia), brown feather; (C) Golden-shouldered parrot (Psephotellus pulcherrimus), brown feather; (D) Kōkako (Callaeas sp.), feather; (E) Passenger pigeon (Ectopistes migratorius), brown feather; (F) Passenger pigeon (Ectopistes migratorius), grey feather; (G) Eskimo curlew (Numenius borealis), dark feather; (H) Eskimo curlew (Numenius borealis), light feather; (I) Ivory-billed woodpecker (Campephilus principalis), feather; (J) Alagoas curassow (Mitu mitu), dark feather; (K) Alagoas curassow (Mitu mitu), down feather; (L) Javan lapwing (Vanellus macropterus), feather.
